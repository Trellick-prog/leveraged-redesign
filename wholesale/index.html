Eligibility criteria and considerations

To qualify and be recognised as a wholesale investor, you need to meet one of the following criteria under the Corporations Act 2001 (Corporations Act).

Wholesale investor tests
TestRequirement
Income testEarned a gross income of $250,000 AUD p.a. or more over the last two financial years
Asset testNet assets exceeding $2,500,000 AUD or more
  • Accountant’s DeclarationIssued under Chapter 7 of the Corporations Act 2001 · PDF

You will need to provide an Accountant’s Declaration issued under Chapter 7 of the Corporations Act 2001. Wholesale customers must provide a new valid certificate every two years. If a customer is no longer wholesale, they may be required to undergo a full responsible lending assessment to determine the suitability of their credit limit and their ability to operate a margin loan product as a retail customer.

Consumer protection

A customer classified as wholesale will generally have less consumer protection than a retail customer. Wholesale criteria may be changed in the future by ASIC, or the lender’s policies on accepting applications and criteria for wholesale applicants may change.

Risks to consider when using a margin loan

Gearing involves risk

It can magnify your returns; however, it may also magnify your losses. The performance of your investments may change, which may result in you earning a lower return or incurring a larger loss than if you had not borrowed to invest.

Loan may become due quickly

Events such as Margin Calls, market disruptions, credit limit exceeds, default events or termination can result in some or all of your loan being due for payment in a short period and/or immediately.

Interest rate changes

An increase in the variable interest rate will increase your borrowing costs and may affect your overall return.
