About us

Established in 1991, we're proud to be a margin lending specialist in Australia, and a wholly owned subsidiary of Bendigo and Adelaide Bank.

The professional’s choice

We understand investors have individual needs and preferences. Our range of products have been specifically developed to provide our clients and partners the flexibility to choose the loan that best aligns with their investment needs and risk appetites.

Why Leveraged?

We understand investors have individual needs, that’s why our experience, market expertise, product solutions and premium service, ensures our partners and clients are well supported in their investment strategies.

  • Connectivity

    Connect with most major online platforms and selected brokers.

  • Acceptable investment list

    A diverse and frequently reviewed investment list, including securities (domestic and international)1, and managed funds2.

  • Specialist solutions

    A choice of multiple margin loan solutions, additional features, and Qantas Points.

  • Range of interest rates

    Offering a range of interest rate options, terms and payments methods. Refer to leveraged.com.au/rates for further information.

  • Dedicated customer service
