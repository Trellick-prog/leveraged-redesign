Leveraged Direct Investment Loan doubles down on 2020 5-star CANSTAR rating

Latest news·6 February 2020

Recognition in this article

Canstar
5-Star Rating, 2020Direct Investment Loan.

This article was published on 6 February 2020 and is kept as an archive. Interest rates, products, features and people mentioned may have changed since then. See current rates and products.

Margin lending specialist Leveraged has again been awarded a 5-Star Rating for its Direct Investment Loan.

Canstar Research found that the Leveraged loan performed strongly on price due to the low interest rates on offer, relative to the other eleven products assessed in the latest 2020 survey. Leveraged’s fixed rates were deemed to be particularly competitive when assessed against margin loan products from nine providers, based on an equally weighted combination of price, features and service.

Head of Leveraged, Lily Elliott said “The Leveraged Direct Investment Loan currently offers one of the lowest advertised rates in the market while also providing the flexibility and service excellence investors looking to build and diversify their portfolio are seeking.

“Asset acquisition and portfolio diversification is made easy with more than 650 acceptable investments and choice of broker while providing great online capability and 24/7 transactions, making the Direct Investment Loan an attractive option for investors to consider.

"Our highly competitive interest rates for loans up to $500,000, combined with an easy online application process with no application or establishment fees and risk management tools allow younger investors to boost their investment power and diversify into a range of asset classes that are available on the ASX", Ms Elliott concluded.

*Ratings are only one factor to consider when choosing a financial product.
