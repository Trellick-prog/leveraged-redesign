Transition completed 1 April 2023

If you have not yet returned your Transition Pack forms to Leveraged, you may experience some limitations on your facility. For questions about the transition, or if you do not wish to continue with Leveraged, call 1300 307 807.

What this means for you

Leveraged was named Money magazine’s Margin Lender of the Year for five consecutive years, from 2020 to 2024.*

Leveraged is a provider of margin lending solutions with over 30 years of experience, 100% based in Australia. Leveraged has previously purchased margin lending portfolios from Macquarie Bank and JBWere. From 1 April 2023, your ANZ Share Investment Loan was transferred to a Leveraged Equities Share Investment Loan. When this happened:

  • Your credit limit will remain the same
  • The loan interest rate schedule will continue to apply
  • Your current fee schedule will remain
  • Any fixed loans will be transferred
  • The Buffer Ratio will increase from 5% to 10%
  • The LVRs (including Diversified) applied to investments in your portfolio will remain unchanged unless otherwise notified by Leveraged

Transfer documents and forms

Please complete any outstanding forms in your Transition Pack and return them to Leveraged in the reply-paid envelope provided. These forms are partly pre-filled with your details; completing and returning them helps ensure the full operation of your Share Investment Loan. For other forms, visit our forms page.

After the Transfer Date your loan moved from ANZ to Leveraged. Both products offer the following features. Leveraged also offers additional benefits, including the ability to earn Qantas Points.

Will I be subject to a responsible lending assessment as part of the transfer?

As your Credit Limit remains unchanged, you will not be required to undertake a responsible lending assessment as part of the transfer in the vast majority of instances. An assessment will typically occur if you apply to increase your Credit Limit or there is a change in your circumstances that requires Leveraged to conduct a review.

How are Guarantors transferred, and will they be notified?

All persons who have guaranteed, indemnified or agreed to assume an obligation in respect of the Borrower’s loan or account are transferred to Leveraged as Guarantors, and their existing obligations and liabilities continue. Leveraged will communicate with Guarantors by letter, and may also send forms if additional information is required.

Where can I find my fee schedule, and are there break fees on fixed rate loans?

Your current fee schedule remains unchanged and is available under ‘Share Investment Loan’ on our Fees page. Fixed rate loans are transferred. Refer to the same fee schedule for information on Early Repayment Costs.

How do I activate Option Transactions, and what happens to open positions?

If you have Option Transactions enabled, you will receive a Leveraged Equities Share Investment Loan Exchange Options Plus Application Form to complete and return. From the Transfer Date, ‘Option Transactions’ are no longer available under your existing ANZ Share Investment Loan terms.

To continue to hold or trade options positions, you must do so on the terms of Leveraged’s Share Investment Loan Exchange Options Plus Agreement (EOPA). Unless you completed, signed and returned the EOPA application form (and any required power of attorney form) before 1 April 2023, Leveraged will attribute a zero value to any open Option Transaction, which will adversely affect your Portfolio Security Value. Depending on the circumstances, this could lead to other adverse consequences such as a margin call.

I have Managed Funds without a Regular Geared Savings Plan. Will these be transferred?

Leveraged will engage with the relevant Fund Managers to ensure Leveraged Equities is listed as the new margin lender. You may be asked to complete some forms to make this change, and we will contact you if so.

How do I apply for additional features?

If you have Option Transactions and/or a Regular Geared Savings Plan, relevant forms will be sent so the features are active upon transition if completed and returned. After the Transfer Date you can apply for Options Transactions, a Regular Geared Savings Plan and the ability to earn Qantas Points. Contact our Customer Service Team on 1300 307 807 or customerservice@leveraged.com.au.

How does the Adelaide Cash Management Account operate?

If you have a Linked Investment Account such as the Adelaide CMA, debit and credit balances can be swept between it and your Loan account to manage your loan more efficiently, for example to reduce interest charges. The sweep function will not be activated, and any credit balance in your CMA cannot be used to reduce your Loan balance or interest charges, unless the Sweep Account Request form was returned by 1 April 2023.

As of 5 December 2022, the interest rate on the Adelaide Cash Management Account was 1.00%. For current terms, read the Adelaide Cash Management Account Product Guide, available from the official transition page; printed or emailed copies are available from Leveraged on request.

Frequently asked questions

When did the acquisition occur?

Bendigo and Adelaide Bank Limited announced in July 2022 it had agreed to acquire the ANZ Investment Lending portfolio. The acquisition allows Bendigo and Adelaide Bank to further grow its margin lending business, Leveraged Equities Limited. View the original ASX announcement.

What information will I receive and how?

The Transition Pack was communicated in two parts:

  • In early February 2023, ANZ issued important information regarding the transfer of your loan to Leveraged, which may require actions from you.
  • Soon after, a letter from Leveraged was posted to you with any relevant forms to complete and return in the reply-paid envelope enclosed.

In late March 2023, Leveraged sent Borrowers their Leveraged Online login details: a Facility Access Code (FAC) by post, and a one-time code (password) by SMS that, together with the FAC, enables login.

We are joint borrowers and received two transition packs. Do we return both?

No. You only need to complete, sign and return the documents in one of the packs. As joint borrowers, please ensure both borrowers sign all forms returned to Leveraged. If you have already returned documents and are unsure whether they were completed correctly, we will contact you if we need more information.

How will I view my account after I transition?

You will no longer be able to access your share investment loan through ANZ My Portfolio. You will view your account through Leveraged Online.

How can I request a withdrawal from my Linked Investment Account?

If you instructed ANZ to transfer your V2 Plus balance to the Adelaide Cash Management Account held as security under your Share Investment Loan with Leveraged, contact the Customer Service Team on 1300 307 807, or log in to Leveraged Online to instruct a transfer to your Nominated Account. Requests previously made through a CMC trading account linked to your ANZ loan are replaced by a link to log in to Leveraged Online.

Will there be any impact to my portfolio or HIN?

Your HIN and Sponsored Holdings are transferred to Leveraged’s Sponsor, Pirie Street Custodian Limited. Your HIN number remains the same, including your holdings.

Where will my EOFY 22/23 statements be available?

End of year statements are available from Leveraged, through Leveraged Online.

What were the transaction cut-off dates?
  • CHESS-eligible transactions (e.g. share transfers): most available until 29 March.
  • Non-CHESS-eligible transactions (e.g. unlisted managed funds): available until 24 March.
  • Changes to your Loan account: most could be made prior to 22 March.

See the list of all cut-off dates. If you would like to make certain changes after transfer (e.g. adding a Borrower or Guarantor), you may be required to apply for the Leveraged Equities Margin Loan.

Can I move back to ANZ?

ANZ no longer supports investment lending products, so once transferred to Leveraged Equities you cannot move back to ANZ Investment Lending.

What if I don’t want to continue with Leveraged?

Refer to the letter sent to you by ANZ in early February 2023, or contact Leveraged on 1300 307 807.
